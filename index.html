Keeping our folk music stories alive.

Folk music is more than a performance. It carries stories, memories, traditions and the voices of local communities. We made this small website to introduce a few folk forms of Maharashtra and encourage people to learn from local artists.

A FEW EXAMPLES

Folk forms of Maharashtra

These are short introductions. Our next step is to add recordings and information collected with permission from local performers.

♫

Lavani

A lively performance tradition known for expressive singing, rhythm and dance. Dholki is often an important part of its music.

Song & dance
✦

Powada

A ballad form that tells stories of bravery and historical events, often performed with strong, energetic singing.

Storytelling
♬

Bharud

A folk performance associated with Sant Eknath, combining music, characters and social or spiritual messages.

Music & message
✺

Gondhal

A traditional ritual performance with singing and instruments, connected with community and devotional practices.

Community tradition

OUR FIELDWORK PLAN

Questions we can ask

  1. How did you learn this folk tradition?
  2. Which instruments are usually used?
  3. Has the tradition changed over the years?
  4. What difficulties do local performers face today?
  5. How can college students help preserve it?
Fieldwork note: We will take notes, ask permission before recording, and verify information with the performer.
